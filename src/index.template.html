Execution & evaluation

The agent’s own work.
Carried forward.

The same agent works in a persistent workspace. Later subtasks consume and revise earlier outputs as evidence and feedback arrive. Reference outputs are not inserted between tasks.

Three views of final delivery

Diagnostic perspectives, not an extra layer of score weights.

A

Artifact quality

Is each deliverable correct, complete, professional, and usable?

C

Cross-file consistency

Do related files agree on facts, values, conclusions, and current status?

P

Package acceptance

Does the complete handoff satisfy the project’s scope and acceptance requirements?

Scoring & reporting details+

All applicable final criteria enter a single aggregate. Signed integer-weighted judgments are summed, divided by the available positive weight, and scaled to 100. Scores are clipped to [0, 100]; a triggered project-level fatal gate sets the score to zero. The benchmark score averages the 11 project scores equally.

A, C, and P identify assessment scopes. Separately normalized scores support diagnosis; no additional A/C/P coefficients are applied to the final score.

GPT-5.6 Luna judges criteria. Experts checked 478 final-delivery decisions for GPT-5.6 Sol–Codex on P01, P05, and P10, agreeing on 446 (93.31%). This validation covers those decisions for one system, not all runs.

Reporting snapshot · 14 October 2026. Scores summarize archived runs under the current integer-weight specification. The weights were revised after the runs; existing judgments are retained where the artifacts, criteria, and evaluation context are unchanged. This reporting snapshot does not constitute new candidate executions.
